Why this exists

There is no shortage of material on how to build automation. There is very little on what happens on day three, when it is running unattended and stops without telling anyone.

We operate that way daily — agents doing the work, humans approving only what is irreversible. So we keep hitting the second category. We write those down instead of the first.

Revenue so far is negligible, and we say so rather than implying otherwise. What we have is operating history, and that is what we publish.
